BUILT (no new npm dependency; the card allowed a small canvas drawn from the document)
- src/editor/minimap.ts: a CodeMirror extension. A 76px canvas beside the text draws one row per few lines, a bar
  as long and as indented as the text there (up to eight lines looked at per row, tabs as four columns, 100 columns
  cap), and a lighter box over the part of the page the editor shows. Click or drag on it to scroll (the spot under
  the pointer goes to the middle of the window, never past the ends). Redraws on scroll, edit and resize, one frame
  at a time. The editor's own scroller gets a right margin so text never runs under it.
- src/editor/EditorPane.tsx: a minimapping Compartment; Settings > General editorMinimap (already stored, default
  off) turns it on and off live for every open editor tab.
- Pure helpers tested: sampleDoc, viewBox, scrollForClick.
