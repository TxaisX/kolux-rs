How it works
- Inside the page, the chord script now sends Mod+F (find), Mod+= / Mod++ (zoomIn), Mod+- (zoomOut),
  Mod+0 (zoomReset), and Ctrl+wheel (Cmd+wheel on a Mac; Alt or Shift with it scrolls) to the app.
  The app's existing browser-chord listener focuses that tab's split and clicks the pane's hidden
  [data-go] button, so the find bar opens on the page that got the key.
- The engine's own zoom keys are off (zoom_hotkeys_enabled(false)); the pane keeps its level, steps
  through 25..500% and sets it again after every load, so reloads and other sites keep it.
- A middle click or Mod+click on a link opens it as another tab behind this one; Shift with either
  brings it to the front; target=_blank and window.open become a front tab (on_new_window, Deny).
  Script-made clicks, cancelled clicks, download links and non-web links are left to the page.
  The T-500 gate still applies to every link opened this way.
