DECISIONS MADE FOR TXAIS (reversible)
- Removal trust = exact set-aside name form + next to its original when on record + parent under a skill folder named in the record.
  The record's own `roots` serve carried entries and start-up; a new delete writes the live scanned folders joined with the carried ones.
- An older record with no `roots` removes nothing: its entries are refused at start-up (logged) and dropped by the next delete; the
  hidden .kolux-deleting-* copies stay on disk for the user to rename back. Only trial-build profiles can hold such a record.
- A refused entry in a not-done record is dropped (logged after the record is cleared), never put back and never removed.
- A readable record that could not be cleared is reported as "its record <path> could not be removed/rewritten (...); try again" and
  retried once per request; the "damaged" advice is kept for parse failures only.
