lane/chat from f1ad80d5, 2026-10-19. No Rust touched (cargo gates skipped). No App.tsx change (no patch).

WHAT THE USER CAN NOW DO
- Switch away from a chat (another screen, or the window minimised) and the chat stops reading its transcript at once: the
  retry timer is cancelled, a read still out is ignored, and the transcript is let go (chat_close). Coming back opens on the
  last good conversation immediately, then one new read replaces it, so turns written meanwhile appear with nothing repeated.
- A chat that begins hidden reads nothing until it is shown.
- If the terminal behind the pane is a different one when the chat comes back, the kept messages are thrown away (the box and its
  draft stay: the draft is kept per pane, not per terminal).
- A read that fails after messages were shown (transcript broken, not found, call rejected, "loading" again) leaves the
  messages in place and keeps trying; the full-pane error shows only when nothing was kept.
