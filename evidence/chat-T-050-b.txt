chat wave 2, T-050-b (VIEW-019): the Agents screen's chat body

What changes for the user (once evidence/chat-FIX-2-App.patch is applied):
  1. A Claude, OpenClaude or Codex session picked on the Agents screen shows as a chat thread with
     its composer once its transcript is found; the terminal behind it is hidden. A "Show terminal"
     switch sits in the chat's header.
  2. Show terminal (switched on) gives that session's raw terminal back, with the switch above it;
     switching it off returns to the chat. No restart; the choice is per session.
  3. While the session launches, or while its transcript cannot be read yet, the chat's line and
     composer sit above the terminal (at most 45% of the height) so a launch prompt stays visible.
  4. A plain shell pane shows its terminal, with no switch and no "Starting..." line (before, a
     shell got "Starting..." forever).
  5. When the chat starts covering the terminal and focus was in the terminal grid, the composer
     takes focus, so typing goes to the chat.
  6. An orchestrator-started session with no display name or title reads in the rail as its
     worker's task label (orchestration worker_labels), never the wrapper; then the folder, then
     "Session n".
