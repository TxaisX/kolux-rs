T-044 (ACCT-012 A lapsed sign-in is flagged outside Settings with Sign in again ·
ACCT-013 A needs-attention card states the reason and offers the one fix that fits)

No real sign-in was used. Every screenshot was taken with stand-in programs named claude.exe and
codex.exe, compiled for this check and put first on the dev app's PATH. The claude stand-in
reports "logged in" for `auth status` only when the account's credentials file holds a made-up
"fresh" marker, and for `auth login` waits 2 seconds and writes such a file. The codex stand-in
writes a fake auth.json (made-up email kim@example.com) for `login` and fails everything else.
Neither opened a browser or contacted anyone. Accounts were seeded straight into the lane's own
profile (sam@example.com, kim@example.com) and removed afterwards; the stand-ins were deleted.
