QUESTION FROM ITEM 1: does plan.ts re-read Codex figures on the codex-accounts event?
No. plan.ts only stores what the pills publish. CodexPill.tsx re-reads codex_usage through its own runner on its schedule and on window focus (2 minute debounce), not on the codex-accounts event. So right after a Codex switch the "Figures for" name follows the new active account at once (it comes from switcher.active), while the figures themselves can lag until the next read. The label can therefore briefly name the new account over the old account's numbers. Left as is: showing the Codex label with a stale read is the lesser evil; T-548-b (per-account reads for Claude too) is the place to make the read follow the event.
