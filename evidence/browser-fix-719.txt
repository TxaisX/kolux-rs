after-list row 719 (T-272-b): permission and download notices raised while a tab's pane is being rebuilt.

Built
- src-tauri/src/browser_notices.rs (new, mod browser::notices): a per-tab pending list. browser_open marks the tab
  "building"; while it is, a permission notice (browser_engine.rs PermissionRequested) or a download notice
  (browser_downloads.rs, keyed by download so progress replaces itself) is held, newest 32 per tab (oldest dropped); a pane silent for 15 s is treated as up (an unpatched or older front end never gets notices held for good).
  A tab with no entry sends at once, as before. browser_close clears the tab's list.
- browser_pane_ready(id): the pane calls it once its page is open; it sends the held notices in order and returns how many.
- src/browser/reopen.ts openPage(): browser_open then browser_pane_ready (a failed announcement is ignored).
  BrowserPane's mount and the rebuild/profile reopen (reopenPage) both go through it.
- Pending patch: evidence/browser-fix-719-lib.rs.patch registers browser::notices::browser_pane_ready in lib.rs
  (apply with git apply; without it an updated pane's call is ignored and notices stay held, bounded at 32).
