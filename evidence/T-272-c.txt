T-272-c: each tab family has its own new-window allowance; "popups blocked" in the opener's toolbar
lane/browser from eebc71ee · 2026-10-20 · Opus 5.5

What changed
- src-tauri/src/browser.rs: the one shared page allowance (five tabs per ten seconds) is now one per
  family. A family is the tab the user opened plus every tab its pages opened; its members share one
  allowance, so a page that copies itself into each new tab still gets five in all, while a storm in
  one tab leaves real clicks in every other tab alone. A page-opened tab joins its opener's family
  when browser_open runs for it: the opener's page was let open an address (the promise, kept ten
  seconds), and the pane opens exactly that address as the new tab (both sides compared after the
  WHATWG address rules, so spelling differences do not split a family). A tab opened any other way
  (by the user, restored after a restart) starts its own family. Opening the same tab id again keeps
  its family; closing a tab leaves the family and the allowance goes with the last member.
  A refusal that a user could have caused (a shown tab, an address a tab may open) emits
  browser-blocked {id}; hidden tabs and refused addresses stay silent as before.
- src/browser/popups.ts (new): the toolbar note's rules ("Popup blocked", then "N popups blocked"
  while it shows; gone ten seconds after the last refusal; the user's own navigation clears it).
- src/browser/BrowserPane.tsx: listens for browser-blocked for its own tab and shows the note as a
  chip beside the zoom badge (data-popups-blocked).
- No App.tsx or lib.rs change: the opener reaches the new tab through the back end's promise, so the
  coordinator hunk the card expected is not needed.
