WHAT THE USER NOW SEES
- When Claude, OpenClaude or Codex asks a multiple-choice question, the chat shows a card above the message box: the question and its
  header, the options (radio buttons, or check boxes when several may be picked) with their descriptions, a "Or type an answer"
  field, and for several questions "Step 1 of 2" with Back, Next, Skip and Submit. Submit types the answer into the agent's terminal
  as the option numbers; while it types the card says "Sending..." with Cancel and locks the choices.
- The card goes when the agent records the answer, when the user types a message or the agent is interrupted (a /clear leaves a user
  row), or when the user dismisses it (x, "answer in the terminal"); the same question asked again stays hidden.
- Keys are dropped (the rest of the answer is not typed) when Cancel is pressed, the pane's terminal changes, the hook stops saying
  "waiting", or another question takes the card's place; the chat then says "The answer was stopped part way. Look at the terminal."
- Select All from the app menu selects the message box's text and Paste puts the clipboard text at its cursor, when the chat box is on
  screen and the focus is not in another text field or a terminal (needs evidence/chat-T-327-b-App.tsx.patch).
