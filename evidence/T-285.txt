WHAT WAS BUILT (new shell-owned src/shell/endAll.ts, 3 vitest in endAll.test.ts; Resource Manager patch)
- Kolux RS runs its terminals inside the app (ConPTY in-process; there is no separate terminal service), so "end every
  session" and "restart the service" both end every terminal session that a fresh read lists at the click. The words
  differ, as the two cards do.
- SET-042 End all: a button in the Resource Manager (status bar memory figure, APP-054) beside Manage Sessions. The click
  reads the sessions fresh and asks: "End every terminal session (N terminal sessions)? Every program running in them stops
  and their panes show that the session ended. A terminal opened meanwhile is left alone; new terminals open straight
  away." / End all / Keep (Keep focused, VIEW-116 style). Confirm ends only the ids the question named that a fresh read
  still lists (manage.tsx's endConfirmed), through terminal_kill per id; Terminals::kill drops the session and its records
  (dirs, marks, keys, history, flow) at once, so leftover state is gone; the pane shows the exit through the usual exit
  event. The report comes from a read after the kills: "N terminal sessions ended; new terminals open straight away." —
  also "No terminal session was left to end; new terminals open straight away." when the click found none (success even
  with nothing left). A session still listed after its kill is named: "1 terminal session did not end (Session 7); the
  rest did. See the diag log under the profile folder."
- SET-060 Restart terminals: the second button, same flow, its own words: "Restart the terminals? Every running terminal
  pane (N) is killed; this cannot be undone. The panes show that their process exited and can be reopened at once." /
  Restart / Keep; report "The terminals were restarted: N ended. Reopen a pane for a fresh shell." Nothing happens before
  the confirm; the app itself keeps running. The "older protocol sessions are kept" rule has nothing to keep in RS.
- T-037 rest: that offer sits in the Resource Manager, asking first (this patch).
