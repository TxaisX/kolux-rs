WHAT THE USER CAN NOW DO
Launching a kit (from Starter kits or the first-run wizard) records the first-kit milestone as soon as
at least one of its AIs starts; Open results on the kit strip, Open results in Recent kit runs, and a
successful Collect results record the first-results milestone. Onboarding keeps each milestone only the
first time (ONB-011, onboarding.rs). The kit results strip carries a tour anchor
(data-tour-anchor="kit-results"; selector KIT_RESULTS_TARGET) that a contextual tour step can point at.
The checklist that shows these ticks is T-333 (ONB-013, not built yet): until it lands the ticks are kept
in the onboarding record and nothing on screen changes.
