What was built
- src/i18n/en.ts: the English catalog (the glossary's plain words and hover lines, the Connect
  cards' lines). A key ending in .hover is hover text; every other key is on-screen text.
- src/i18n/lookup.ts: t(key, values) gives the text in the language in force, from its pack when
  the pack is loaded and has the text, English otherwise. registerPack(lang, loader) makes a pack
  available; it is fetched only the first time its language is needed (once, even when asked

  onPackLoaded tells a screen to draw again. Names are passed in as {name} values, never
  translated. packJargon(pack) runs the ONB-036 plain-words guard over a pack's on-screen texts.
