clippy --all-targets -D warnings: clean.

How it works: every window a page asks for (window.open, target=_blank) passes the T-500 gate and
then one sliding-window allowance (5 per 10 s) shared by all browser pages, so a page and the tabs
it opened draw on the same allowance; past it the request is denied and nothing opens. A link the
user opens with a middle click or Mod+click is the user's own action and is not counted.
