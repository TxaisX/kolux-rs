T-043-b (ACCT-005 browser sign-in for managed Claude accounts, ACCT-006 upkeep left to Claude Code)

No real sign-in was used. Every screenshot was taken with a stand-in program named claude.exe,
compiled for this check and put first on the dev app's PATH. It prints "Stand-in claude (not
Claude Code)", answers `auth login` by waiting a set number of seconds and then writing a fake
.credentials.json into CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR, and answers `auth status --json` with a made-up email
(sam@example.com) and organisation (Stand-in Org). No request went to Anthropic, and no
browser page was opened. The stand-in and its throwaway repo were deleted after the check.
