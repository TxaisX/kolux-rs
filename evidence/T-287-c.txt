T-287-c · council AFTER (MERGE 38 review): kolux diagnostics memory's committed total is honest when a process could not be read · 2026-10-13 · lane/cli (after 3aee3c1)

What changed: a process tree (the app's own row or a session's) that holds any process whose committed figure could not be read now shows a dash for committed and says "(n of m could not be read)", instead of a partial sum that passed for the whole. The Total row does the same: "– MB ... 7 processes  (2 of 7 could not be read)". JSON: every row gains unreadable (a count); the answer gains committed_total (null when any process could not be read) and unreadable (the count across the app and every session). An older app without committed_total: the CLI sums the rows itself and shows a dash as soon as a row with processes has no figure.
Unreadable branch: proven by the unit tests (a stubbed process with no committed figure in a session tree; a stubbed answer with 2 of 7 unreadable). In the live run below every process of the app and its session could be opened (unreadable 0), so the live output shows the all-read branch; no real unopenable process was in a Kolux tree on this PC (System-owned processes are never children of the app or its shells).
Tests: added resources::tests::t287c_committed_total_is_null_when_any_process_could_not_be_read; changed resources::tests::cli041_committed_adds_up_per_tree_and_stays_unknown_where_nothing_reported_it (session 5's tree with one unreadable process is now unknown, unreadable 1, where it used to be a partial 4) and cli::tests::cli041_diagnostics_memory_shows_committed_beside_resident_and_passes_it_through (the 2-of-7 text, the older-app fallback).
