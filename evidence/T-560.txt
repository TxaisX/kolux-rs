Follow-up 2026-10-13 (after the no-launch rule; no app was started for this part)
- Found by the new self-test: Node 24's rmSync maxRetries does not retry EPERM on a folder another
  process holds open (it failed at 364 ms while the holder lived to 1715 ms, and the same folder deleted
  fine right after). The 1534e66 retry therefore did nothing for EPERM.
- removeTemp(dir, pid, retries): waits up to 10 s for the app's pid to exit, then its own loop retries
  EPERM / EBUSY / ENOTEMPTY with waits 0.5 s, 1 s ... 2.5 s, and returns the error code instead of
  throwing. It is used by the final cleanup (WARN line) and the setup's first clean (setup error).
- --offscreen: the config the smoke hands Tauri copies the app's own window entries from
  tauri.conf.json (a list is replaced whole on merge) and sets x/y -32000, center false and
  skipTaskbar true. The app's show() then shows the window off-screen, the second-screen move is
  skipped, and CDP is untouched.
- --self-test: launches nothing.
  `node scripts/evidence/smoke.mjs --self-test --offscreen --temp G:/Temp/sessions-w1/selftest`:
    PASS self-test: a held folder with no retries is reported, not thrown: EPERM
