Coordinator default under DT-C27 (Txais may overrule, after-list row): quitting stops every pane and
a crash takes them with it, so every worker still starting, running or stopping when an earlier
Kolux process ended comes back after restart as "cut short, outcome unknown" with recovery steps.

Deferred under DT-C27 (named on the PLAN line): running workers re-attached without a second brief,
settled worker tabs kept across a restart without waking, and one background terminal service
with an instance lock and a check that the terminal layer loads. They need panes that outlive the
app, which DT-C27 defers until the old app is retired.
