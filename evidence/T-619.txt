T-619 evidence, 2026-10-30, lane cli (worktree /home/user/kolux-lanes/cli, branch lane/cli)
Closing a busy terminal never freezes the other terminals: the background terminal service closes a pane's
console, and resizes a pane, outside the one lock every pane's output needs. Audit R06-terminal-process.2
(pty_service.rs:322, high).

--- what changed (src-tauri/src/pty_service.rs)
:336-352  op "kill": the Term leaves `terms` (and its flow leaves `flows`, released) under the service lock;
    the Term is dropped after the lock is let go, which ends it (PtySession::drop -> kill: input closed,
    TerminateProcess, master dropped = ClosePseudoConsole, then the pane's job). This is what on_exit and
    end_all already did. While ClosePseudoConsole waits for the output to drain, the pump's on_output can
    take the lock again, so the drain finishes and no other pane stalls. The killed pane's reader no
    longer waits for a pane that is gone (its flow is released and removed, as the window side does).
    A second kill of the same id answers "no terminal <id> in this service", which pty_link's
    end_unowned already reads as "already gone".
:328-334  op "resize": the master and its size keeper are cloned out under the lock and the resize runs
    after it is let go (resize_outside, :167-176: PtySession::resize's own steps, the size recorded under
    the master's lock as before). A resize that waits on the pane's output no longer holds the lock.
    The exit event still reaches every window: on_exit broadcasts it by id, whether or not the Term is
    still listed (and it no longer is once killed).

   The service's copy of terminal.rs's row784 tests (registry_free_during_stalled_close): a fake session

2. :1297 tests::t619_a_resize_waiting_on_its_terminal_does_not_hold_the_service_lock
   A real terminal is spawned; the test holds its master's own lock (where a real ResizePseudoConsole
   waits), a resize is sent on another thread, and `list` must answer within 2 s; once the master is let
   go the resize completes and the size is 100 columns.
   Red (025eda2): the resize ran inside with_term and blocked on the master under the service lock:
   assert!(free, "list waited behind a resize that waits on its terminal") fails.
   (A fake MasterPty that stalls inside resize would need anyhow::Error in its signature, and anyhow is not
   a direct dependency; holding the master's lock models the same wait without one.)
