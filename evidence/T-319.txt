How it works: after every finished load the back end asks the page for its first <link rel=icon>
(else /favicon.ico), fetches it itself (8 s timeout, through the T-500 gate) and sends it to the pane
as a data: address, since the app's own pages may not load web images. The pane shows it in place
of the globe; a load within the same site keeps the icon, another site drops it back to the globe
until the new icon arrives, and a reload asks again, so an icon that failed is retried. The title
and New Tab naming were already there (browser-title, App's tab name); the toolbar's Stop button
already spins while loading (BROW-005).
