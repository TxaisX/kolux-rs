Tests changed, said loudly: src/editor/buffers.test.ts had two tests that asserted the OLD rule
(a mixed file re-read with CRLF, drawing LF lines joined). They were updated to the DT-C57 rule:
- "an LF log that a CRLF writer appends to ... keeps every line on reload" now asserts line 11 is
  "added by Add-Content" (its own line) and fileText(state) === mixed; it previously asserted
  sliceDoc() === mixed (bytes only, drawing not checked).
- "a CRLF note ... gains LF-only lines ..." now expects the separator to become LF, 5 doc lines and
  fileText(state) === withLf; it previously expected separator CRLF (which drew "x\ny" as one line).
Neither test was weakened: each now checks more of the user-visible result (drawing + bytes) than before.
