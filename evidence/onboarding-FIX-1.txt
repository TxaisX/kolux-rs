FINDING (real bug found by item 4, fixed in the same commit)
  scm.rs gh_budget names the budgets "REST", "Search" and "GraphQL" (capitalised) and drops any budget without a numeric
  limit, remaining and reset. readSeen looked for the lowercase name "search", so against the real Rust answer the task
  search always read as failed and the Connect integrations task step could never fold to done. The old stand-in answered
  with a lowercase key list and empty budgets, which hid this. readSeen now matches the name without regard to case.
