- No keys assigned (the shipped state): the grid shows no minus or plus session control; the
  pane header's plus is New tab, the strip at the top is the layout picker (T-101-b-no-stepper.png).

  was refused for this lane; it was not retried (DT-R39). Unit tests stand in:
  keymap.test.ts "TERM-002: the session shortcuts ship with no key, and once assigned they fire"
  (no chord by default, an unassigned chord matches nothing, Mod+Alt+Equal and Mod+Alt+Minus from
  a keybindings file fire sessions.add and sessions.remove), and seats.test.ts "TERM-002: add then
  remove returns the grid to its previous pane count; a started session is the one asked about".
- The App.tsx handlers for sessions.add and sessions.remove (addChooser, removeSession) were built
  in T-101-a and T-103-a; a started session is closed through closeTab, which asks first while
  Settings > Terminal & sessions > "Ask before closing a tab that is running a program" is on.
