What changed
- src-tauri/src/browser_session.rs (new, pub mod session): each tab's trail (the addresses it
  loaded and the one it is on, 50 at most, the title) in browser-tabs.json under the app's data
  folder (version 1, atomic save). noted(): a reload changes nothing, a step back or forward along
  the trail moves the mark, a new address cuts the forward part; step(): the trail's neighbour.
  tidy(): trails without an address, with a mark past the end, or whose worktree folder is gone are
  dropped at load; a file of another version or shape (an older build) reads as empty and is
  rewritten at the next save. Live: opening(id, url, shown) takes the saved trail for the tab's id
  once per run (the pane keys survive a restart in the saved layout, PROJ-067) and gives the address
  to start on, or about:blank plus a pending load when the tab is not on screen; take_pending()
  when it is shown; noted_load/noted_title/placed follow the page; step_tab for Back/Forward;
  forget on close. The budget (BROW-021): Page {id, worktree, seen, live, spared} and
  to_unload(pages, BUDGET = 3): the worktree whose tab was on screen last is active and never
  counted; hidden worktrees holding a live page count; past the budget the least recently visited
  ones give up their live pages, but a page with a download running (downloads::downloading) or
  one an agent drove in the last 10 minutes (drove/driven, marked in drive()) is spared; tabs
  without a worktree are neither counted nor unloaded. unloading(id) marks the page pending at
  its address; the web view goes to about:blank, the trail stays.
- src-tauri/src/browser.rs: pub mod session; browser_open asks session::opening for the start
  address and sends the saved title to the tab strip; on_page_load finished and the title change
  feed the trail; browser_bounds(shown) loads a pending address before showing the view;
  browser_go back|forward and the command line's Act::Go go through step_history: the engine is
  asked (navigation.canGoBack / canGoForward) and runs its own history.back()/forward() when it
  can, else the trail's neighbour is loaded (a tab back from a restart has no engine history);
  browser_close forgets the trail; browser_tab tells the trail its worktree and, when a tab comes
  on screen, runs the budget and blanks the pages it names; drive() marks the tab an agent drove.
- src-tauri/src/browser_downloads.rs: downloading() loses its dead-code allowance (the budget
  reads it now).
- evidence/browser-T-238-b-lib.rs.patch: regenerated (browser_suggest registered after
  browser_go), so it applies with or without browser-T-242-lib.rs's line in place.
- No TypeScript change: the pane already reports its worktree and whether it is shown

Ceilings (coordinator defaults; Txais may overrule, rows on the after-list)
- The budget is 3 hidden worktrees with live pages, counted by worktree, not bytes; no setting
  (the card: on by default with no settings page). "Active" is the worktree whose browser tab was
  on screen last; a worktree with no browser tab never counts.
- Back/Forward on a tab back from a restart walk the trail by loading the neighbour (the engine's
  own list cannot be refilled), so the engine's list grows with each such step.
- An unloaded or not-yet-shown tab reads as blank to the pane (its address bar is empty and
  `kolux tab list` shows no address) until it is shown; the tab strip keeps the saved title.
- The card's "saved active tab missing → first valid browser tab" and "tabs of deleted worktrees
  dropped" are the saved layout's (PROJ-067: a tab whose folder is gone is dropped, the active tab
  returns only while it exists); the trail store drops its own records for gone worktrees too.
- Floating workspaces: their browser tabs are panes with keys like any other, so their trails
  come back the same way (not proven live).

LIVE PROOF PENDING (batched; the app launches are the proof lane's)
1. Open three browser tabs, navigate twice in one, quit, relaunch: three tabs return (the selected
   one loads, the others stay blank until clicked, their names kept); Back in the navigated tab
   goes to its earlier page.
2. Delete a worktree that had a browser tab, relaunch: no tab for it comes back.
3. Open pages in ten worktrees and visit each in turn: the oldest hidden ones go blank (their
   titles stay); go back to one: its selected page reloads.
4. Run `kolux goto` on a page in a hidden worktree, then visit more worktrees: that page stays
   loaded; a tab with a download running stays loaded.
