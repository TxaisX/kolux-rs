T-583 (status-hooks lane, 2026-10-30): the hook line never blocks, so status never freezes and the safety pause is never skipped
=================================================================================================================================
Audit item 6, R02-hooks-status.3, ponytail notes hooks.rs:707, automations.rs:1206 and automations.rs:1005.

THE PROBLEM (old code, hooks.rs serve/handle)
- One listener thread answered every hook connection in turn, with a 2 s timeout per read, not per request, and no cap
  on the request line or headers: a local client that sent nothing held every hook for 2 s, one that trickled a byte
  under 2 s held them for good, and an endless header line grew memory without limit.
- Before writing the reply, handle() ran resume::on_stop (store lock), saved::note (store write),
  automations::follow (store lock, then reading the pane's text and up to 8 MB of transcript) and automations::on_finish,
  which for an "agent finished" automation ran its precheck (60 s by default, up to 10 minutes) right there; then it took
  the main store lock again to read the settings for the protected-command answer (SAFE-009).
- The hook script's curl gives up after 1.5 s (COMPAT 3.1) and prints nothing, so meanwhile every pane's status froze
  and a protected command got no "ask": the agent ran it without the pause.

- src-tauri/src/hooks.rs:59-64 MAX_HEAD (64 KiB for the request line and headers) and READ_WITHIN (2 s for the whole
  request, from the accept).
- src-tauri/src/hooks.rs:287-301 parse_request reads the head through take(MAX_HEAD); a head cut off at the cap, or

ORDER
Under the static ORDER lock (hooks.rs handle(), the inner block after `let req = Arc::new(req);`) sit only three quick
steps: the status machine (answer), the window event (raise) and the hand-over to the after-reply queue (a channel
send). So the window and saved::note hear each pane's steps in the order the machine took them, and the queue runs the
store work in that same order. The reply body (reply_body: protected_custom::check, the protected-attempt record and
the mask of a paused command) is worked out after the lock is dropped, on the connection's own thread; protected_custom
keeps its own STORE mutex, and a call's PreToolUse/PostToolUse/Stop for one session are ordered by Claude itself (it
waits for each reply). Events a pane sends at the same moment (parallel helper agents) are taken in parallel.

PR #13 REVIEW (2026-10-30)
Blocking: accept() gave every connection a bare std::thread::spawn with no cap; a flood or a thread limit makes spawn
panic, the accept thread dies, and status and every protected-command check stop for the session (hooks fail open).
Nit: queue()'s worker died with a panicking after_reply job, and later jobs were dropped.
Fix (src-tauri/src/hooks.rs): accept() spawns with thread::Builder; on Err the closure, its stream and its place are
dropped and the loop goes on. MAX_IN_FLIGHT = 64 connections at once, counted by an AtomicUsize per listener; an
InFlight guard gives the place back when the handler ends. A connection over the cap is closed at once, unanswered (the
hook script then goes on without Kolux), logged once per flood. queue() runs each job under catch_unwind and keeps going.
