What the user can now do
- Type into the chat's message box on the Agents screen and press Enter: the text is typed into
  the agent's own terminal, shows as a pending bubble until the transcript records the turn, and a
  failed write keeps the bubble with "This message did not arrive. Look at the terminal."
- Shift+Enter makes a new line; the box grows to at most 8 lines; Up/Down from the first/last line
  step through what was sent before; an IME composition never sends; each pane keeps its own draft.
- Keys typed while focus is elsewhere in the chat land in the box.
- The chat follows new messages while you are at the bottom, holds still when you scroll up, and
  shows "Jump to latest" until you go back down; Top goes to the start.
