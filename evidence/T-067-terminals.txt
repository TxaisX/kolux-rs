== TERM-065 · copy, never silently (TerminalPane attachCustomKeyEventHandler + src/terminal/copy.ts)
Clipboard seeded with `t067-before` (Set-Clipboard) before each case; read back with Get-Clipboard -Raw.
1. Ctrl+Shift+C with nothing selected: no notice ([role=status] absent), clipboard still `t067-before`,
   focus stayed in the pane textarea.  -> an empty selection never reports success.
2. Ctrl+Shift+A (select all) then Ctrl+Shift+C: clipboard = 162 chars containing marker-A.
3. Ctrl+C with a selection: clipboard replaced by the selection.
4. Ctrl+C with nothing selected: the key reached the shell: the row shows `echo never-sent^C`, the next
   command `echo after-interrupt` ran alone, clipboard unchanged.  -> plain Ctrl+C is the interrupt.
5. navigator.clipboard.writeText replaced by a rejecting stub ("locked by another program"); two
   Ctrl+Shift+C presses 1 s apart: ONE notice "The clipboard refused the copy (locked by another
   program). The selection is still here; try again.", the 3 .xterm-selection divs remained,
   activeElement still the pane textarea, clipboard unchanged.  -> failure notice, throttled, selection kept.
Unit: src/terminal/copy.test.ts (3 tests), src/terminal/renderer.test.ts (10 tests).

== TERM-103 · GPU rendering, cap of six, plain fallback
- Setting `terminalGpu` (Settings > Appearance > Terminal drawing > "Draw terminals with the GPU"),
  default on; off = every terminal plain. Screenshot T-067-gpu-switch.png: dark appearance, new look,
  maximised 1920, the switch on.
- terminalGpu=true, relaunch with the 12 saved terminals (checkB.json): pane header tips end with
  "GPU renderer" for 6 terminals (the hidden home pane included) and "plain renderer" for the other 6:
  the seventh and later terminals took the plain path. renderer.ts gpuPool {max: 6, live}; a freed slot
  (dispose or WebGL context loss) is handed to the next terminal; a WebGL failure counts nothing.
- Glyph cache reset: clearGlyphs() after a paste over 2048 chars and when the 5 s heartbeat is more
  than 30 s late (watchResume: machine sleep/resume). Unit-tested; not exercised live.
- Synchronized frames: xterm 6 handles mode 2026 itself (grep of node_modules/@xterm/xterm/lib for
  2026); revealing a hidden pane runs fit + refresh. No extra code.
- "Opening 10 terminals never shows blank panes": NOT MET in this proof, for a reason outside the
  renderer. Shells started by the app sometimes never run: the powershell.exe child sits at 1 thread,
  0.00 CPU, Wait/Executive (process initialisation), its conhost at 4 threads 0 CPU, for minutes;
  the pane says Ready but stays blank and ignores terminal_write and terminal_resize. Seen for 1 of 11
  panes in the plain run, for 10 of the 12 shells restored at the GPU relaunch (only the last two
  created ran), and for 3 pane-less `terminal_spawn` calls over CDP (two back to back, one alone).
  The registry mutex is NOT held (terminal_busy / get_settings answer at once during the stall). The
  machine ran twelve lanes at the time. Not root-caused in this task; it belongs with T-166
  (recover after sleep/wake, typing never stuck, terminal_write async) which is the next task of this lane.
  The panes whose shell did run drew text on both paths (6 GPU + 2 live shells in the GPU run; 10 of 11
  in the plain run).
