T-073 · EDIT-003: an editor tab notices that its file changed on disk (2026-10-13, lane/look on 2e6821b)

ADVISORY, NOT THE GUARD
The banner is advisory. It runs off a cheap stamp (modified time + size, file_stamps in files.rs),
which a same-size rewrite inside one poll interval, or a tool that puts the old modified time back,
slips past. The guard stays the save path's hash check from T-073-a: file_write takes the hash the
tab loaded and refuses on mismatch, whatever the stamp says. Proven below (live, step "guard") and
in tests: files.rs t073_council_the_hash_check_refuses_even_when_the_stamp_says_unchanged and
diskChange.test.ts "council: the banner is advisory, the save's hash check is the guard".

RULES AND WHERE EACH IS PROVEN
- Kolux's own saves never raise the banner: live "save" (3 s after Keep mine and after a plain
  Mod+S: banner null); test "Kolux's own save remembers the stamp it wrote".
- A clean tab reloads silently: live "quiet"; test "a clean tab takes the disk copy quietly".
- A dirty tab gets the banner (Reload / Keep my edits / Compare): live "banner"; render test.
- Reload is undoable (toast Undo + undo history): live "banner" (Undo restored the edits);
  test "the toast's Undo applies only while the buffer still holds what Reload brought in".
- Reload always fetches fresh and drops a late overlapping read: tests "an earlier read that
  finishes late is thrown away" and "Reload always fetches fresh and overtakes a quiet reload"
  (unit only; the race is not reproducible by hand).
- Keep hides the banner until the next change: live "compare" (4.5 s after Keep: banner null)
  then "readfail" (the next change raised it again); test "Keep my edits hides the banner".
- Compare: disk left, buffer right, its own Reload and Keep: live "compare" + T-073-compare.png.
- A read failure shows on the Compare side: live "readfail".
- A background tab reloads when it comes forward; a hidden dirty tab is not touched (the check
  only runs for the tab in front): live "front".
- Poll every 2 s for the tab in front and on window focus: POLL_MS = 2000 in diskChange.ts; the
  banner showed 357 to 1793 ms after each shell write.
- Autosave held while the banner is up, restored drafts compared with disk after a restart: not
  buildable yet (autosave and drafts are T-177); bannerUp() in diskChange.ts is the hook, tested
  in "a tab with edits keeps its text, raises the banner and holds autosave". Left on T-073-d.
