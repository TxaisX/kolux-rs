  [--source auto|transcript|terminal] [--cursor C] [--limit 1..100]`. auto reads the worker's own transcript
  (the transcript_path its pane's hooks reported, read through the T-325 chat::Follow reader), else its
  terminal output (the T-134 Terminals::read lines) labelled "terminal output" with fallback=true, else, for a

  A worker with no terminal refuses --source terminal (no_terminal) but always answers --source transcript.

  status 75 and prints "orchestration ask --resume <id>"; a resumed wait never hands back; a dev build or
  another system waits on. exit_status() maps ASK+handedBack to 75, failure to 1, else 0.
- cli.rs dispatch(): any terminal.* command whose --terminal is a dispatch id (not term-N) resolves through
  orchestration::terminal_for to the worker's terminal; a worker without one is refused with no_terminal
  and the hint "worker-read --dispatch X --source transcript".
