  deletable-only filter, in-use rows never deletable, treemap with the tail folded into one block.
- Folder workspaces: worst PR check among attached and nested worktrees, dismissed PRs left out,
  root cannot be deleted/forgotten (src/projects/folderWorkspace.ts).
