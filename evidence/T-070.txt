T-070 · USAGE-023 usage figures refresh on a timer and ride out errors
Date: 2026-10-13 · lane look · checkout G:/Dev/kolux-rs-lanes/look (lane/look, on 7ae48db) · dev build, new look, dark

The scheduler (src/usage/refresh.ts, pure functions of the clock and the last result; one runner per provider)
  Timer       every 5 minutes (EVERY_MS); runs regardless of the focus backoff, honours a provider's retry-after.
  Focus       a window focus or a visibility change to visible reads again unless a read began under 30 s ago
              (MOMENTS_MS); the first focus after a delayed start reads once.
  Backoff     while reads keep failing a focus waits 30 s, 60 s, 120 s, then 300 s (the cap = the timer period)
              after the last try; one answer clears it.
  Manual      refreshAll() (the Tower's Refresh button) reads every provider at once, past the debounce and past
              any retry-after; nothing starts a second read while one is in flight.
  Isolation   claude and codex each have their own runner and state; a lagging or failing one never stalls the
              other. A failed read keeps the figures already shown (Codex publishes an error state only when it
              never had any) and raises one steady error mark (TroubleMark, data-usage-trouble="1") with no
              loading spinner; a Claude status-line report arriving during the start-up read is kept, because
              an empty answer is "nothing new", never a replacement. A busy Codex is asked again in 3 s.
