What is built
- src-tauri/src/schedule.rs: an RRULE may now carry INTERVAL (1 to 1000), COUNT (1 to 100000),
  UNTIL (a date takes in the whole day), BYSETPOS (1 to 366, negative from the end), WKST, numbered
  weekdays in BYDAY (1MO, -1FR, 4TH, up to 53) and a DTSTART before it, written
  DTSTART:20261013T090000 (the automation's wall clock), with Z (UTC) or DTSTART;TZID=Zone:... .
  Such a rule keeps its parts (Rule) and is walked period by period from DTSTART: the hours, days,
  weeks (from WKST), months or years INTERVAL apart; each period's days must match every part
  (BYMONTH, BYMONTHDAY, BYDAY, as RFC 5545 says, not cron's either-day rule), are crossed with the
  hours and minutes, BYSETPOS picks from them, then COUNT and UNTIL end the series. A plain rule
  (FREQ with BYMINUTE, BYHOUR, BYDAY, BYMONTHDAY, BYMONTH) stays the five cron fields as before.
- Parts a rule leaves out come from DTSTART: its time, its weekday for WEEKLY, its day for
  MONTHLY, its month and day for YEARLY.
- Refused when saving, with a reason: INTERVAL or COUNT without DTSTART, COUNT with UNTIL, a
  numbered weekday outside MONTHLY or YEARLY, BYSETPOS=0, 0MO, a DTSTART not in the
  20261013T090000 form, and parts not supported (BYYEARDAY, BYWEEKNO, BYSECOND).
- ponytail (in the module header): no BYYEARDAY, BYWEEKNO or BYSECOND yet.
