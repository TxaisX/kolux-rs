ux fix 2: the top-bar bell and the window title show how many panes need you (lane ux, 2026-10-25)

Root cause: TopBar rendered the bell as a bare icon with a fixed label and App set document.title only from AppInfo,
so nothing outside Board/Agents/Inbox/tab badge signalled a waiting permission. Spec check: spec/04 STAT-050 and
spec/05 VIEW-003 describe the bell as opening the Inbox and say nothing about a count; no DECISIONS entry says it
deliberately shows none; the window title has no spec card. Not intended, fixed.

Fix: needsYouCount (src/terminal/status.ts) counts open, non-browser panes in the existing `needs` record
(useAgentStatusState), the same set nextNeedsYou reads. App passes it to TopBar (needsYou) and to windowTitle
(src/app-info.ts): "(2) Kolux RS 0.1.0" while above 0. The bell gets a .bell-count badge (--working-icon amber, the
needs-you colour) and the label "Inbox, 2 need you" / "Inbox, 1 needs you" through src/i18n/en.ts (+ the 5 packs).
