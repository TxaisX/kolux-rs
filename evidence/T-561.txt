T-561 (council MERGE 52 FIX-SOON, lane/sessions from 1534e66): a partial committed sum says so
2026-10-13

Before: resources.rs total() added up committed memory only over the processes that reported a
figure, so a tree where some processes could not be opened showed the partial sum as if it were the whole.

Now:
- Usage carries committed_unread, the number of `processes` with no committed figure (JSON "committed_unread").
- The Resource Manager's committed cell shows the figure. When the sum is partial, it adds
  "(n of m could not be read)" under the figure, and the cell's title gives the same text.
- None read is still the plain dash (T-287-c's wording: a dash or "some unknown").
- An older payload without the count reads as whole.

Tests:
- cargo: resources::tests::merge52_a_partial_committed_sum_counts_the_processes_that_could_not_be_read
  (shell read, 2 descendants not: Some(4) with 2 of 3 unread; none read: None with 1 of 1; app 0 unread;
  serialised as committed_unread).
- vitest: "MERGE 52: a committed sum that left processes out says how many could not be read".
- vitest: "MERGE 52: the committed cell shows the partial note under the figure, nothing extra when whole"
  (server-rendered cell: "4.0 MB" plus "(2 of 3 could not be read)"; whole has no note; none is the dash).
