T-585 (status-hooks lane, 2026-10-30): a helper agent whose stop never arrives does not keep its pane "working" forever
=====================================================================================================================
Audit R02-hooks-status.2 (src-tauri/src/status.rs:257). Cards STAT-026, STAT-028.

THE PROBLEM
Esc while Claude runs a helper agent (a sub-agent) stops the turn and the helper, and Claude sends neither the
helper's SubagentStop nor the lead's Stop. Kolux refuses to conclude that Esc while a child works (STAT-028), and a
child left the roster only by its own SubagentStop, a session boundary, its terminal ending or a deny. So the stopped
helper stayed "working" on the roster, resolved() kept answering "working" after every later turn's Stop, no done
notification came and keep-awake held, until /clear, a resume or closing the terminal.

WHAT IS CLEARED, AND WHY NOT AT EVERY STOP
The task text suggested clearing the roster when the lead's turn stops or a new prompt starts. Clearing it at the
lead's Stop would break STAT-026's ticked P0 box "The lead ends its turn while a subagent still works -> the lead shows
working until the child finishes" (and its tests in status.rs, e.g.
the_lead_stays_working_while_a_child_works_and_a_teammate_waiting_raises_needs_you and
a_child_stop_announces_its_own_reply_and_a_gone_child_never_returns), and clearing every working child at a new prompt
would drop a teammate or background helper still at work, whose later permission questions would then never raise
"needs you" (STAT-026: a child already cleared away never comes back). So only what the Esc stopped is cleared: the
children that were working when that Esc was refused and have not been heard from since, at the next prompt.

WHAT CHANGED (src-tauri/src/status.rs)
- :206-209 Session.escaped: the children working when an Escape could not be concluded.
- :542-555 mark_cancel: when the cancel is refused while the lead works, the working children are noted there.
- :303-306 fold: any event carrying a child's agent_id (its tool events, its own start or stop) takes it off that note:
  the Esc did not stop it.
- :444-446 UserPromptSubmit: the noted children still "working" leave the roster before the new turn begins.
STAT-028 is unchanged: the Esc itself still concludes nothing while a child works.

   at the next prompt; with no Esc, a helper that outlives the lead's Stop keeps the pane working across a new prompt
   and the next Stop (STAT-026 box 1).

LIMITS AND WHAT IS LEFT
- A helper whose stop is lost some other way (no Esc), or a background helper silent from an Esc until the next prompt,
  is not covered: clearing those needs a signal Kolux does not have (the audit's idea, Claude's Stop background_tasks
  list naming live helpers, is not confirmed to list sub-agents). Question for Txais in the lane result.
- Live proof on Windows: start a Claude task that spawns a sub-agent, press Esc while it runs, send a new prompt; when
  that turn ends the pane reads Done and the done notification fires.
