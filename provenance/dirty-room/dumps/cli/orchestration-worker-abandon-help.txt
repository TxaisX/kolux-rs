kolux orchestration worker-abandon

Usage: kolux orchestration worker-abandon --dispatch <dispatch_id> [--retry-request <id>] [--json]

Fence a worker without claiming its process stopped

Options:
  --help                 Show this help message
  --json                 Emit machine-readable JSON
  --pairing-code
  --environment
  --dispatch
  --retry-request

Notes:
  Retains all possibly-live resources and performs no process or filesystem action.
